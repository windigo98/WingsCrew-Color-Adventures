# Vector-redo Grok Imagine line art

Hand-drawn replacements for the 48 older vector-looking pictures.
These are source PNGs only. They are not traced or shipped in the app yet.

Source: Grok Imagine, 2:3, 1152×1728, grayscale PNG.
Adults pages were run through `thicken_light.py` (light MinFilter line boost).
Little Kids and Big Kids pages were converted to clean grayscale with a light paper threshold (values ≥ 240 become pure white). No line thickening.
